# Glossary

What the words of the shop's docs mean.

## order

Also: orders
See: orders.create, orders-db

What the shopper buys: the items of the cart, as `orders.create` stores them in the orders table.

How an order moves through the shop is in [the order guide](docs/orders.md#how-an-order-moves).

## cart

See: shop-ui.cart

The list of items the shopper picks before buying.
